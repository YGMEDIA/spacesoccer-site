Controls

Hold. Charge.
Release.

One stick and a few round buttons that change their job depending on whether you have the ball. Shots, passes and crosses charge with a ring that runs from cyan to orange. Try it: hold a button.

Move

Push further to go faster, hold Sprint for full speed.

Shoot

Hold to charge, release to shoot. A quick tap chips the ball over the keeper.

Pass

Goes to the teammate you are facing. Hold longer for a sharper pass.

Cross

Curls the ball into the box. Hold longer to send it further.

Sprint

Hold for full speed. With the stick alone you jog.

Tackle

Win the ball standing up. Timing matters, a miss costs you a moment.

Slide

From distance. Tap to slide in, hold to jockey the ball carrier.

Switch

Jumps to the teammate best placed for the ball. Press again to keep switching.

Sprint

Chase back. Your teammates run into position on their own.

Prefer a gamepad? Controllers are supported.
